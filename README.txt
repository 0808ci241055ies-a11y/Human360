V14 SMART NUTRITION UPDATES
- Removed the Resting Heart Rate (bpm) input completely.
- Heart-rate training zones are estimated from age-predicted maximum heart rate and fitness level; these are general estimates, not medical measurements.
- Added clearer profile validation for age, height, weight and exercise duration.
- Smart Nutrition Engine now includes expanded Indian meal suggestions such as roti-dal-sabzi, dal tadka, bajra roti, sambar rice, kadhi-rice, soya curry, khichdi, thepla, chilla and more.
- Personalization controls include diet preference, meals per day, budget, cooking time, regional meal style, ingredient exclusions and nutrition focus.
- Meal recommendations are scored against goal and selected priorities; ingredient exclusions are respected.
- Achievements section removed; dashboard, nutrition, adaptive workouts, progress, goals, analytics and printable health report retained.

IMPORTANT
This is a front-end prototype. Demo accounts and data are stored in browser localStorage; passwords are not securely protected. Do not use real sensitive health or login information. Nutrition and exercise estimates are for general wellness education, not diagnosis or treatment.
